Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know the sarees are handwoven?

Every LAKIRA saree is sourced directly from weaving artisans. Each product page lists the fabric (linen, silk, cotton, Jamdani, Banarasi and more) and weave details so you know exactly what you are buying.

Do slight colour differences occur?

Handwoven fabric can vary slightly in shade and texture from batch to batch, and screens display colour differently. These small variations are part of the handloom character.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location and the product. Please see our Shipping & Delivery page for current timelines and charges.

Can I return or exchange a saree?

Our Refund & Exchange policy explains eligibility and the steps to request a return or exchange. You can read it on the Refund & Exchange page or contact us for help.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Cash on Delivery may be offered on eligible orders and can require mobile number verification by OTP at checkout.

How should I care for linen and silk sarees?

Dry-clean silk sarees and store them folded in a muslin cloth. Linen can be gently hand-washed in cold water or dry-cleaned, and softens beautifully with every wear. Avoid direct sunlight when drying.

Can I visit a store or get styling help?

Yes. Visit us in Bengaluru or call/WhatsApp us and our team will help you choose a saree, blouse or dupatta.
